# Technical Training

We share our academic expertise and industry experience with your organization's technical teams. We do not offer formal certification programs; our goal is knowledge transfer and practical competency development.

**Not certification, knowledge transfer. Not theory, practice.**

---

## Our Training Approach

As ARTEK, our training programs are based on practice-oriented knowledge transfer, drawing from our academic background and industry experience.

Key characteristics of our training:

- **Knowledge Transfer Focused:** Content that combines theoretical knowledge with practical applications, directly contributing to your business processes
- **Customized Programs:** Training programs designed according to your organization's sector, current competency level, and specific needs
- **Application-Oriented:** Workshop sessions conducted through your own projects and real-world scenarios
- **Mentoring Support:** Continuous communication opportunity where you can get answers to your questions even after the training

> **Note:** For training requiring formal certification (ISO certification, mandatory regulatory training, etc.), we provide referrals to authorized institutions.

## How Does the Process Work?

> [!NOTE]
> Each training program is customized according to your organization's specific needs. Instead of ready-made package programs, we offer content designed together with you.

### 1. Needs Analysis

We assess your organization's current competency level, goals, and training needs together. We identify which areas require development and set priorities.

### 2. Program Design

We design a customized training program based on the needs analysis. We consider your preferences regarding content, format, duration, and scheduling.

### 3. Training Implementation

We conduct training according to the established program. We reinforce theoretical knowledge with practical applications and work on real projects.

### 4. Evaluation and Follow-up

After training, we evaluate the outcomes and plan additional sessions if necessary. We provide mentoring support for your questions.

> [!WARNING]
> No formal certificate is awarded at the end of our training. We can issue a certificate of attendance upon request, but this certificate does not substitute for formal certification.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are certificates provided at the end of training?

No, official certificates are not provided at the end of our training programs. We are not an official certification body or continuing education center. Upon request, we can issue a participation certificate, but this document does not replace official certification. We provide referrals to authorized organizations for training that requires official certification.

### Are your training programs regulatory mandatory training?

No, the training we offer is not within the scope of legally mandatory training. You need to work with authorized organizations for mandatory training such as occupational health and safety or environmental regulations. Our training programs are entirely voluntary participation-based, aimed at competency development.

### Are training programs ready-made packages?

No, each training program is customized according to your enterprise's specific needs. After evaluating your needs, current competency level, and goals in the preliminary meeting, we design a program specifically for you. This way, we focus on the topics you truly need.

### Is remote training possible?

Yes, we can conduct most of our training through online platforms. We offer an effective learning experience remotely with live sessions, screen sharing, and interactive applications. For workshops and hands-on training, we can plan face-to-face sessions when necessary.

### Is there a minimum number of participants?

While a minimum number of participants is recommended for in-house training and workshops, it is not mandatory. Our one-on-one mentorship programs can be conducted with a single person. We adapt the training format and content according to the number of participants.

### What sources are the training contents based on?

Our training contents are based on our academic background, sectoral experience, and current practices. We follow the current regulations and guidelines of institutions such as TÜBİTAK and KOSGEB. We also share practical knowledge gained from our own project experiences and consultancy processes.

### How is training pricing done?

Pricing is determined according to the training format, duration, number of participants, and content scope. We provide you with a customized proposal after the needs analysis. Our initial consultation is free of charge, and we evaluate your needs in detail during this meeting.
