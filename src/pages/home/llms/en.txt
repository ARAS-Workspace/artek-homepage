# ARTEK Innovation

ARTEK İnovasyon Ar-Ge Sanayi ve Ticaret Limited Şirketi — from specialized sectoral consultancy to custom software development, ARTEK delivers comprehensive solutions with an engineering vision throughout enterprises' digital transformation and innovation journey. Based in Kütahya, Türkiye.

**Everything in one file:** [llms-full.txt](https://www.artek.tc/llms-full.txt?locale=en) — every page listed below, concatenated into a single document (Turkish is the bare URL).

## Services

- **Center Establishment Consultancy** — end-to-end consultancy for R&D/Design Center applications under Law No. 5746: infrastructure and certification.
- **Project Consultancy** — project preparation, application, and management support for TÜBİTAK, KOSGEB, Development Agencies, and international funds.
- **Technical Training** — corporate training programs in R&D management, project documentation, technical writing, and engineering processes.
- **Enterprise Software Solutions** — custom enterprise software that digitizes operational processes.
- **Web & Mobile Applications** — scalable web and mobile solutions built with modern technologies.
- **Data Analytics & Artificial Intelligence** — BI- and AI-backed analytical solutions that strengthen data-driven decision-making.

## Frequently Asked Questions

- **What services do you offer?** Two main categories: consultancy (R&D/Design Center establishment, TÜBİTAK/KOSGEB/Development Agency project consultancy, technical training) and software development (enterprise software, web/mobile applications, data analytics and AI).
- **What is the engagement process?** Free initial consultation → needs and technical-requirements analysis → tailored solution and proposal → joint project execution after approval.
- **Can consultancy and software services be combined?** Yes — the services complement each other (e.g. an R&D Center setup can include building its project-management or data-analytics infrastructure).
- **Do you work remotely?** Yes; evaluation, consultancy, development, and training can run online, with site visits scheduled when needed.
- **How does pricing work?** Flexible project-based or duration-based models; the initial consultation is free.
